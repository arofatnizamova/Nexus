Solutions in waterproofing, flooring and protective coatings

For over 25 years, we have been developing and manufacturing polyurethane, epoxy, and polyurea resins for construction, renovation, and industrial applications. From our facilities, we distribute to more than 70 countries, following strict research, production, and quality control procedures to ensure the highest standards and maximum customer satisfaction.

About Us

More than 25 years of experience in the development and manufacturing of liquid membranes for construction and industry.

We develop and manufacture our own polyurea, polyurethane and epoxy resins for all types of waterproofing and flooring coating projects at our facilities in L’Hospitalet de l’Infant, Tarragona. We also produce protective membranes for industrial applications.

Additionally, we provide extensive technical advisory experience, supporting projects at every stage. We offer training, certifications, and warranties for our products and systems.
